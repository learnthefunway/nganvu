Round 1 — Your training budget
How many training hours does ELCA sponsor per employee per year?
40 hours per headcount per year. Total company-wide: 16,000 hours (~400 employees), planned and monitored by L&D.
Round 2 — What counts toward 40hrs?
Which activities count toward your 40hr budget?
Counts ✓: Internal Training, External Training, Self-training Roadmap, Online learning via 3rd party. Does NOT count ✗: Boost Up Programs (sponsored separately), Certifications.
Round 3 — Sort into pillars
Which activity belongs in which pillar?
Formal: Internal Training, External Training, Self-training Roadmap, Boost Up Programs. Informal: LinkedIn Learning, Pluralsight, GoFluent, Bookboon. Development: Certifications, Competitions, Expert Activities.
Round 4 — Logging your training
You just completed an internal training. What should you do next?
Log it in Vertec using code 2223-103-31. Every training must be logged with the correct Vertec code. Use the Confluence reference link if unsure.
Round 5 — Getting certified
You want L&D to cover your certification fees. What's your first step?
Register with XC and make sure your name is on the list BEFORE getting certified. Order: Register with XC → Get certified → Submit payment request + certification to L&D.
